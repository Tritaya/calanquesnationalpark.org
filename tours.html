How this table is built (and the disclosure)

We scraped every tour GetYourGuide files under the Calanques National Park, Marseille, Cassis and La Ciotat, then removed anything not genuinely about this coast — category pages here get padded with unrelated bestsellers, and we caught trips to Corsica's Calanques de Piana and the Verdon Gorge wearing the "calanques" label. The main table keeps tours with 50+ reviews and a 4.3★ floor, ranked by review volume; the rest stay visible in the niche list above rather than quietly deleted. Per-row notes are written by us from park regulations and operator smallprint — including which boats never stop, and which "calanques" trips run on the Côte Bleue instead.
